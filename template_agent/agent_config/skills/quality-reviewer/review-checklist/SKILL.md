# Quality Review Checklist

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Completeness (weight: 30%)
- Does the report address ALL sub-questions from the research plan?
- Are there obvious angles or perspectives that are missing?
- Is the executive summary a faithful representation of the full report?
- Are key takeaways supported by the body content?

### 2. Source Quality (weight: 25%)
- Are sources recent enough for the topic? (Technology: <12 months; general: <24 months)
- Are sources authoritative? (Official docs, research papers, established publications)
- Is there source diversity? (Not all from one publication or perspective)
- Are there at least 5 distinct sources for MODERATE+ topics?

### 3. Balance & Objectivity (weight: 20%)
- Are multiple perspectives represented?
- Does the report acknowledge limitations or counterarguments?
- Is the language neutral rather than promotional?
- Are claims properly attributed rather than stated as absolute facts?

### 4. Accuracy (weight: 25%)
- Do 1-2 spot-check searches to verify specific claims in the report.
- Are statistics and data points correctly cited?
- Are technical terms used correctly?
- Are comparisons fair and based on equivalent criteria?

## Scoring
- **9-10**: Excellent — publication-ready
- **7-8**: Good — minor improvements possible but not required
- **5-6**: Needs improvement — specific gaps should be addressed
- **Below 5**: Major revision needed

## Verdict Decision Framework

### COMPLETE — deliver the report
- Overall score >= 7
- No critical gaps in topic coverage
- Sources are adequate in quality and quantity
- No factual errors found in spot-checks

### NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH — loop back
- Overall score < 7, OR
- A major angle of the topic is completely missing, OR
- Sources are insufficient (<3 for MODERATE topics), OR
- Spot-check found a factual error that undermines a key finding

## When Recommending Additional Research
Be specific about what is needed:
- BAD: "The report needs more information about AI."
- GOOD: "The report lacks coverage of regulatory frameworks for AI in healthcare. Suggested search: 'FDA AI medical device regulation 2025'"

Limit additional research to 2-3 focused sub-questions — avoid scope creep.
